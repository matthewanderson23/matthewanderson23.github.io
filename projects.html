Autonomous Motorsports Purdue

2018 — 2019

Co-founder and Hardware Project Manager. Led a hardware team of 3 electrical engineers building an autonomous go-kart for the evGrandPrix Autonomous event, developing electrical subsystems and control algorithms interfacing LiDAR and camera with drivetrain and steering. Designed a PCB featuring an STM32F1 and oversaw firmware for kart telemetry and a LoRa-based emergency stop, independent of the main autonomy stack.
